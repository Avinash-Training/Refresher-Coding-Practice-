## Key Concepts Covered

- **Console I/O:** `Console.Write`, `Console.ReadLine`, `Console.WriteLine`
- **Primitive Types:** `int`, `double`, `string`, `bool`
- **Arithmetic Operators:** `+`, `-`, `*`, `/`, `%`
- **Math Functions:** `Math.Round()`, `Math.Pow()`, `Math.Min()`
- **Input Validation:** `double.TryParse()`, `int.TryParse()`
- **Control Flow:** `if/else if/else`, `while`, `switch`
- **OOP Concepts (Hard):** `interface`, multiple classes, class properties, factory pattern
- **Business Rules:** Negative value rejection, range checks, overflow prevention
